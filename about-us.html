We don’t just talk drinks - we shape the narrative.

As a boutique content and consulting studio, Drinks & Destinations also collaborates with leading Indian and international wine, spirits, and beer brands to craft impactful strategies for:

  • Brand storytelling and editorial content
  • Social media strategy and influencer outreach
  • Trade and consumer tastings
  • Staff trainings and masterclasses
  • Event curation and judging for competitions
  • Product Development & Brand Strategy

Drinks & Destinations is an award-winning platform that brings you sharp insights, curated stories, and fresh perspectives from the world of beverages, luxury travel, and hospitality. From news and reviews to tastings, podcasts, and expert interviews—this is where the spirit of discovery meets the art of drinking.

Founder Rojita Tiwari

At the heart of it all is founder Rojita Tiwari an independent writer, educator, consultant, and drinks judge with with more than two decades of experience in the alco-bev industry. She has led editorial teams, developed market strategy for top brands, and contributed to globally respected drinks publications.

Rojita is a WSET Level 3 Wine and Level 2 Spirits certified professional, twice listed among the Top Ten Women of Wine in India by the Indian Wine Academy, Most Influential F&B Industry Professional 2020, the recipient of the international Wine Travel Awards 2023, and Ambassador of the Industry Award 2023 by Spiritz Magazine.

She has curated and judged landmark events like:

  • Celebrating India’s 50 Finest Wines (2014)
  • Spirits Selection by CMB (Since 2015)
  • Concours Mondial de Bruxelles Wine Competition (Since 2014)
  • India Wine Awards (2017) – Technical Director
  • Asian Wine & Spirits Silk Route, Beijing (2016)
  • Multiple national and international wine, cocktail and spirits competitions
